About me

Why Information technology?

I first became interested in Information Technology at a young age because we ran a computer shop, which is where I became computer literate and started troubleshooting computers. Spending so much time around systems, I became deeply fascinated by how video games worked under the hood and how hardware and software interacted. Having used technologies since I was in grade school, I built a strong foundation of knowledge early on.

As I grew older and began working on more hands-on technical projects, that pivotal thought—"I want to do this professionally"—crossed my mind and solidified my career path. Because technology surrounds us and shapes how we live and work every day, I could see the massive impact I could make as an IT professional.

I pursued my Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at Datamex College of Saint Adeline, where I am expanding my skills in programming, hardware, and modern tech solutions. I am very excited about learning more throughout my journey and building a successful career in the tech industry.
